# Copy-Based Landing-Page Family Refinement

## Scope

This release adds five new patient-facing copy directions to every active practice: **Sleep Pattern Clarity**, **A Gentler Sleep Check**, **Shared-Night Conversation**, **Sleep Symptom Check**, and **Local Sleep Next Step**. Each direction uses practice-specific GoHighLevel survey routing, matched outcomes, policy links, the configured About the Dentist section, and the approved `Request a consultation` action.

## Refinement Loop One — Symptom Recognition and Patient Safety

The first review focused on whether each direction could be understood quickly without suggesting a diagnosis, a personal risk estimate, or a guaranteed treatment outcome.

| Direction | Retained patient purpose | Refinement result |
|---|---|---|
| Sleep Pattern Clarity | Connect tired mornings, snoring, and low focus without alarm. | The hero leads with a closer look rather than a risk claim; the recognition copy explicitly states that a symptom is not a diagnosis. |
| A Gentler Sleep Check | Reach visitors who have normalized fatigue or noisy sleep. | The page uses reassuring language and a low-pressure survey handoff rather than urgency or guilt. |
| Shared-Night Conversation | Address partner-noticed sleep disruption respectfully. | The framing explicitly removes blame and treats partner observations as useful information, not proof of a condition. |
| Sleep Symptom Check | Give a direct, quick self-recognition route. | The copy asks visitors to consider the full pattern and states that the survey does not diagnose sleep apnea. |
| Local Sleep Next Step | Make a local consultation feel practical and approachable. | The page asks the visitor to share what they notice and avoids assuming the needed treatment. |

All five directions use the same safe care path:

> **Notice a pattern → discuss whether evaluation is appropriate → consider individualized options only after clinical assessment.**

The oral-appliance sections were retained only where they explain that a custom appliance **may** be discussed for some adults after appropriate evaluation. Each section states that suitability is individualized and that an oral appliance is not right for everyone.

No fear-forward statistics, individual risk estimates, diagnosis claims, emergency-style calls to action, guarantees, testimonials, or retired no-cost consultation wording were added.

## Refinement Loop Two — Visual Hierarchy and Treatment Context

The second review checked how the five concepts scan as landing pages rather than as long-form education. The guide icon is present only on the new copy-based pages. It is decorative, non-interactive, and appears above the hero headline without reintroducing the removed campaign-label row. The same open-guide marker appears in the protected admin navigation, copy-family summary, and every related Page Index row.

Each LP retains the shared responsive reading pattern: a focused hero, four icon-led recognition rows, a three-card reasons section, a short oral-appliance explanation, profile context, and the assigned survey handoff. On small screens, the hero marker scales down without crowding the headline, and the guidance cards collapse into readable single-column rows.

The appliance context is intentionally tailored by direction rather than duplicated verbatim. The calm and local-care pages emphasize evaluation and individualized options; the shared-night page emphasizes partner observations without blame; and the symptom-check page reinforces that a survey does not diagnose sleep apnea. Every direction keeps a clear clinical safeguard that a custom appliance is considered only after appropriate evaluation and may not be suitable for everyone.
