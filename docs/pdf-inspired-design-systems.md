## Required Deviations from the PDF

The following PDF elements are deliberately not carried into production because they conflict with the approved universal-language policy: CPAP-alternative messaging, insurance or Medicare coverage claims, “qualification” language, forms/embeds, treatment-process claims, sample clinical credentials, and unapproved testimonials.

## Shared Mobile Requirements

Every additional variant retains the 414×896 mobile-first hierarchy: a visible top phone control, a single hero CTA, clear full-card symptom recognition controls, provider and review placeholders, chat access, legal links, and non-diagnostic disclosure. Desktop retains the same content but adopts the individual concept’s layout treatment.
