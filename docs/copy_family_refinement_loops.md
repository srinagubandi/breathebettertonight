# Copy-Based LP Refinement Loops

## Loop one — concise symptom recognition and supportive conversion language

The five new patient LP families now use shorter hero subheads, recognition introductions, and reasons-to-discuss content. The revision removes duplicated phrases, avoids implying a diagnosis from a pattern, and retains each direction’s intended patient posture: calm clarity, gentle reflection, partner respect, direct symptom recognition, and local next-step support.

The partner direction now refers to a shared pattern rather than assigning fault. The direct and local directions now make the action sequence more direct: notice the pattern, complete a short survey, and discuss an appropriate next step.

## Loop two — evaluation-first treatment context and mobile reading clarity

The treatment sections now use shorter titles, concise three-step labels, and simpler supporting sentences. Every direction preserves the same clinical boundary: a survey does not diagnose; evaluation guides the next step; and a custom oral appliance may be one option for some adults only when appropriate.

The result removes repeated “sleep testing and clinical assessment” phrasing while retaining the safeguards that treatment is individualized and that an oral appliance is not right for everyone.
